# Warm ODE confirmation protocol

Freeze the benchmark executable, model bytes and descriptions, baseline and feature runtime bytes, and the complete 24-case WASM matrix. Record SHA-256 hashes before taking samples. Retain every case, including initial-state-only requests. Earlier development measurements are exploratory and excluded from the confirmation estimates.

Run 32 fresh processes on one pinned CPU. Each of eight chronological groups has three baseline/feature comparisons and one baseline/baseline control in shuffled order. There are 12 forward and 12 reverse comparisons, and four of each orientation for the sham controls. Seed 29 fixes these assignments, the order of each group and the per-process permutation of all 24 jobs. Keep the same CPU, warm-up, persistent compilation-cache policy and thread CPU clock for every trial. Each process instantiates both runtimes and checks output bits and solver counters for every case. It measures three ABBA blocks per case. Case order, process count and analysis are fixed before observation. A failed invocation invalidates completion of that experiment. Keep its logs. A subsequent experiment uses a new output directory.

For each case and process, divide the sum of the two feature times by the sum of the two baseline times within each ABBA block. Normalize reverse assignments before taking the median of the three log ratios. Average process effects within each orientation, weight the two orientations equally, and exponentiate. Every process supplies one 24-dimensional observation. Report all case estimates and their orientation-specific estimates.

Generate 50,000 bootstrap draws with NumPy PCG64 seed 29, separately for comparisons and controls. Sample complete process vectors with replacement within each orientation. For each case estimate its standard error from the two independent orientation strata, with equal orientation weights. For each draw take the maximum across cases of the absolute centered bootstrap effect divided by its observed standard error. The 95th percentile of these maxima supplies the simultaneous critical value for all 24 cases on the log scale. Exponentiate the interval endpoints. When a case has no observed variation, report that fact; its zero width cannot establish performance beyond the sample. Report the sham control family separately as a diagnostic for bias and resolution. Never use controls to subtract a measured slowdown or set a tolerance post hoc.

A simultaneous lower bound above one is evidence of a slowdown. An interval spanning one leaves the direction unresolved and its upper endpoint quantifies the remaining uncertainty. A strict zero-margin claim for all tested cases needs every upper bound at or below one with valid controls. A sham interval excluding one flags the corresponding measurement for investigation. Results characterize these frozen workloads, runtime versions and host. They do not establish performance for every possible model.
