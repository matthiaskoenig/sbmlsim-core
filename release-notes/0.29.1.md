# sbmlsim-core 0.29.1

The steps of the integrator no longer end at a step which leaves the time where it was.

## Fixes

- **A step which leaves the time where it was is no stall** (#109). The output of the steps of the integrator (`Output::IntegratorSteps`, with and without events) ended with `the integrator stalled` at the first step which returned the time it started at. CVODE takes a step whose size is below the resolution of the time (its warning `t + h = t`) and grows the step again: on the rapamycin model of pkdb_models (Kelly1999, a dose every day for 35 days, `rtol 1e-10`, `atol 1e-16`) the first step after a dose from day 23 on (33120 min) returned the time of the dose and the next one advanced, which libroadrunner simulated and the engine refused. A stall is now 100 steps in a row which do not advance the time (`STILL_STEPS`), as the steps of Zeno towards a right hand side which is not finite still are. Kelly1999 runs in 1.1 s on the engine and agrees with libroadrunner to 4.3e-10 relative.
