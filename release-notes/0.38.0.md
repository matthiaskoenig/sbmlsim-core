# sbmlsim-core 0.38.0

PEtab v2 runs in Rust: a problem is read, simulated, evaluated and fitted in the core, with the whole PEtab test suite passing (#142), the validation problems of sbmlsim's extension evaluate the likelihood of their validation data (#146), and the live demo of the documentation runs the published 0.37.0 (#141).

## PEtab v2 (#142)

The new crate `sbmlsim-petab` (`crates/petab`) executes a problem of PEtab v2 on the
engine, with no python and no sympy on the way:

- **Reader.** `PetabProblem::read` reads the YAML file, the SBML models and the tables
  (conditions, experiments, observables, measurements, parameters, mapping) from their
  contents by name, so it touches no file system and runs in a browser as well.
- **Math.** The math of the tables is parsed with the grammar of PEtab into the math of
  SBML, which the compiler and the engine evaluate as they evaluate a model.
- **Plan.** `Plan::new` translates a problem into requests of `simulation/1`: observables,
  noise formulas and the values of conditions after the start are assignment rules of
  parameters added to the model, the parameter table and the first period the
  pre-initialization, a period at `-inf` the steady state before the start, a measurement at
  `inf` the steady state after the end, and later conditions changes at their times.
- **Evaluation.** `llh`, `chi2`, the log priors of all eleven distributions of PEtab v2,
  truncated at the bounds, and the unnormalized log posterior, with the densities of
  `normal`, `log-normal`, `laplace` and `log-laplace` noise.
- **Executor and fit** (feature `native`). `Executor` compiles and simulates the plan on the
  engine and is a problem of `sbmlsim-fit`, so its optimizers, Fisher information and
  profiles fit a problem of PEtab; its cost is the objective of PEtab plus `cost_offset`.
  What the fit cannot carry (priors of the kinds `cauchy`, `chisquare`, `exponential`,
  `gamma`, `rayleigh`, a first period which is no linear form of the estimated parameters, a
  bound which is not finite) is refused with its reason, while the evaluation runs.
- **Command line.** `sbmlsim-core petab problem.yaml [--simulations FILE] [--fit]` evaluates a
  problem at its nominal values, writes its simulation table and fits it.
- **Python.** `sbmlsim_core.petab.Problem` (`from_yaml`, `from_files`, `evaluate`, `llh`,
  `requests`, `fit`, `fit_problem`) is the same problem.
- **WebAssembly.** `compiler.wasm` built with the feature `petab` of `sbmlsim-wasm` carries
  `petab_open`, `petab_model`, `petab_requests`, `petab_evaluate` and `petab_close`, the
  problem read, planned and evaluated in the page and its simulations run by the engine of
  the host. The module of the npm package is still built without it.
- **Test suite.** All 149 cases of the PEtab test suite at the commit sbmlsim pins pass (31
  of PEtab v2, 20 of PEtab v1 converted to v2, 98 of the math), the pass rate of sbmlsim in
  python; the fit is held to the objective of PEtab on every case it carries. CI downloads
  the cases of PEtab v2 and of the math and runs them against a baseline in both directions.
- The fit of the pharmacokinetics of hydrochlorothiazide of pkdb_models (777 measurements,
  59 experiments) evaluates as in sbmlsim, to `1e-12` of the log-likelihood at tight
  tolerances, in 0.1 s.
- `sbmlsim_compiler::compile_document` compiles a document `sbmlsim_sbml::read` gave, e.g.
  one a caller added parameters and rules to.

See [PEtab](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/petab/).

## The extension of sbmlsim (#146)

- The validation problem of a fit (`role: validation`, the problem of sbmlsim's
  `to_petab_problems` which holds the validation data at the fitted parameters) has its
  validation data as the data of its likelihood, so its log-likelihood is the one of the
  validation data, as in sbmlsim; the training data of every other problem are unchanged. The
  validation problem of the PK fit of hydrochlorothiazide agrees with sbmlsim to `1.4e-11`.

## Fits (#142)

- A measurement at the time `inf` reads the row of the steady state after the end. Before,
  the distance of `inf` to `inf` was no number and the measurement read the first row of its
  condition.

## Documentation (#141)

- The live demo of the documentation runs the published npm package 0.37.0.

## Behaviour changes

- A measurement of a fit at the time `inf` reads the steady state after the end instead of
  the first row of its condition (#142).
