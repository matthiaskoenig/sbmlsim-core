# sbmlsim-core 0.44.0

A species of a model can be driven: it follows a path its caller prescribes instead of its rate, and an accumulator counts the amount the model exchanged with it. Every target compiles driven species the same way, `model.wasm` with the engine and its `Stepper`, the native model and the QFunction of the C target and the batched kernel, which becomes `kernel/2` (#175). This is the coupling of the cells of a tissue to their blood: a transport solver computes the blood, the cells follow it and give back what they took up or released. The distributions and special functions of scipy which sbmlsim still called, and the Fisher statistics of a stored decomposition, are functions of `sbmlsim_core.stats` in Rust, python and JavaScript (#176). They are what sbmlsim needs to compute no statistic with scipy any more.

## Driven species (#175)

```bash
sbmlsim-core compile cell.xml --driven S_ext,P_ext -o cell-driven
sbmlsim-core compile cell.xml --kernel --driven S_ext,P_ext -o cell-kernel
```

```rust
let options = CompileOptions { driven: vec!["S_ext".into()], ..CompileOptions::default() };
let description = compile(&sbml, &options)?.description;
let (d, t_ref) = (&description.driven[0], description.t_ref.unwrap());
// [S_ext] falls from 1 mM by 0.01 mM/min over the interval [t0, t1]
stepper.set_parameters(&[(d.value, 1.0), (d.slope, -0.01), (t_ref, t0)])?;
let mut y = stepper.state().to_vec();
y[d.exchange as usize] = 0.0;
stepper.advance(&y, t0, t1)?;
let exchanged = stepper.state()[d.exchange as usize]; // mmol, negative for what the cell took
```

- **The path.** A driven species `X` leaves the states; wherever the math read `X` it reads
  `X__value + X__slope * (time - driven__t_ref)`, three new parameters which the caller sets
  for every interval it advances. The path is the concentration of `X`, or its amount with
  `hasOnlySubstanceUnits`. Initially `X__value` is the initial value of `X` and the slope and
  the reference time are 0, so a model nobody drives keeps `X` at its initial value.
- **The accumulator.** The new state `X__exchange` (an amount) integrates the amount rate `X`
  had, with the path in place of `X`. Written 0 before an advance, it holds the amount the model
  gave to `X` over the interval after it (negative for what it took).
- **Every entry point.** `--driven ID[,ID]` of `sbmlsim-core compile` (with and without
  `--kernel`), `CompileOptions::driven` and `KernelOptions::driven` of `sbmlsim_compiler`,
  `compile_sbml(sbml, driven=[...])` of python, `compiler.compile(sbml, { driven })` and
  `compiler.compileKernel(sbml, { driven })` of JavaScript, and `sbmlsim_c::generate_with` and
  `generate_qfunction_with` of the C target. `model.json` lists the driven species (`driven`,
  with the indices of `value`, `slope` and `exchange`) and the index of `driven__t_ref`
  (`t_ref`).
- **The stepper.** `Stepper::set_parameters(&[(index, value)])` sets parameters between two
  advances, the paths of the driven species or any other settable parameter. The model computes
  again what depends on them, the constants of its optimizer and its assignment rules.
- **The QFunction and the kernel.** The paths are parameters of each point, so their fields and
  arrays are those of an undriven model; every call computes the constants of each point from
  its parameters, so a path may change between the calls. The paths read the time, so the
  QFunction and the kernel of a driven model carry the derivative of the right hand side in the
  time, which keeps RODAS4 at order 4 on a steep path.
- **Refused**, naming the species: an id which is no species, a boundary condition or constant
  species, the target of a rule or an event assignment, a species in a compartment of a
  changing size, a species which `rateOf` or a `delay` reads, a species given twice and one
  whose new ids the model already has.
- **Accuracy.** On the cell model of `testdata/models` with `S_ext` and `P_ext` driven, 200
  points with random paths: the QFunction and the kernel at 1e-8 and 1e-11 agree with a stepper
  at 1e-12 and 1e-14 within `4e-6 |y| + 4e-9`; at the tissue tolerances 1e-6 and 1e-9 the
  accumulators are within `4e-6 s + 4e-9`, `s` the amounts of the cell they balance. The kernel
  takes the steps of the QFunction, the driven ones included.

See [Driven species](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/semantics/#driven-species).

## Distributions and special functions (#176)

```python
from sbmlsim_core import stats

stats.norm_ppf([0.025, 0.975])     # array([-1.95996398,  1.95996398])
stats.t_ppf(0.975, [3, 10])        # broadcast against the degrees of freedom
stats.chi2_ppf(0.95, 1)            # 3.841458820694124
stats.erf(0.5), stats.gamma(4.5)
```

| function | scipy | accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| `norm_cdf`, `norm_ppf` | `scipy.stats.norm` (standard) | `1e-13` relative |
| `t_cdf`, `t_ppf` | `scipy.stats.t` | `1e-10` relative |
| `chi2_cdf` | `scipy.stats.chi2.cdf` | `1e-13` times the condition, against mpmath at 40 digits |
| `chi2_ppf` | `scipy.stats.chi2.ppf` | `1e-13` against mpmath (largest error found 3.7e-14) |
| `erf` | `scipy.special.erf` | `1e-15` against mpmath |
| `gamma` | `scipy.special.gamma` | `1e-13` against mpmath, factorials exact |

- **The edges of scipy.** Not a number, the infinities, arguments outside a domain and signed
  zeros give what scipy gives, to the bit. The value broadcasts against the degrees of freedom
  as numpy broadcasts, with scipy's `ValueError` for shapes which do not; a number gives a
  float, an array an array of its shape.
- **The incomplete gamma function.** The crate gains the regularized incomplete gamma function
  and its inverse (`special::gamma_p`, `gamma_q`, `gamma_p_inv`, in Rust), the base of the
  chi-squared distribution: the series of P, the continued fraction of Q, scipy's series of Q
  for a small parameter, closed forms at 1/2 and 1 and the uniform expansion of Temme from
  `a = 1e6` on. Its oracle is mpmath, since scipy's `gammainc` is off by `1e-8` relative eight
  standard deviations below the mean at `a = 1e6`.
- **JavaScript.** The same functions in `sbmlsim-core/stats` (`stats.normPpf`, `stats.chi2Ppf`,
  ...), held bit for bit to the python by 1147 calls of the fixture.

See [Statistics](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/stats/).

## The Fisher statistics of a stored decomposition (#176)

- `fisher_decomposition(information)` gives the eigenvalues, largest first, and the
  pseudo-inverse of one eigendecomposition.
- `fisher_statistics_of_decomposition(spectrum, inverse, cost, n, likelihood=False,
  rank_tolerance=...)` gives the statistics of `fisher_statistics` from a given decomposition,
  as `FisherInformation` of sbmlsim derives them from a record: over the decompositions of LAPACK
  (`eigvalsh`, `pinv`) of earlier records to the bit.
- `fisher_statistics(m)` is `fisher_statistics_of_decomposition(*fisher_decomposition(m))` to the
  bit.

## Breaking

- **The batched kernel is `kernel/2`** (was `kernel/1`): `KERNEL_CONTRACT` of `sbmlsim-abi` and
  of `js/kernel.mjs` is `"kernel/2"`; compile a kernel of 0.41.0 to 0.43.0 again. The module,
  its exports and its arrays are unchanged.
- **`KernelDescription`.** Its fields are those of `kernel/1`. The driven species and the
  reference time of a kernel are in the description of its model, `model.driven` and
  `model.t_ref` of `kernel.json` (`KernelDescription::model`, a `ModelDescription`, in Rust),
  absent without driven species; a reader of `kernel.json` takes them from there.
- **`compiler.wasm`.** `compile(pointer, len, options, options_len, out)` and
  `compile_kernel(pointer, len, options, options_len, out)` take their options as JSON,
  `compile` the object `{"optimize", "jacobian", "parameter_jacobian", "driven"}` and
  `compile_kernel` `{"driven"}`, each field optional with its default, an empty buffer for all
  defaults and an unknown field refused; `compile_with_options` is removed. `js/compile.mjs`
  does this for a page, so only a direct caller of the module changes.
- **Rust.** `CompileOptions` is no longer `Copy`: its new field `driven` is a `Vec<String>`, so
  a caller clones it where it copied it. `sbmlsim_compiler::compile_kernel(sbml, &options)`
  takes `KernelOptions` (`KernelOptions::default()` drives nothing).
  `sbmlsim_wasm::compile_model(sbml, &ModelOptions)` replaces `compile_model(sbml, optimize,
  jacobian)` and `compile_model_with_options`, and `compile_kernel_model` takes
  `&KernelOptions`.
- **`js/compile.mjs`.** `compile` refuses an `optimize` which is no boolean with a
  `TypeError`, as it refuses the other options, instead of converting it.

## Behaviour changes

- `beta_inc` with a large first and a second parameter below one near the upper end
  (`a >= 100`, `b < 1`, `x > 0.7`) was off by up to `9e-8` relative
  (`beta_inc(1e3, 1e-8, 0.9999)`); it is now within `1.2e-14` of scipy. The bits of `beta_inc`,
  `t_cdf` and `t_ppf` change in that region; every other function keeps its bits.
