# sbmlsim-core 0.21.0

A curve of a fit can carry an infinite standard deviation, a measurement which carries no information, so a problem with such a point is a fit of the engine and no longer refused.

## A measurement of an infinite standard deviation carries no information (#69)

The `noise` of a curve of `fit/1` is the standard deviation of every measurement of it, and the engine refused every value which was not positive and finite. A caller whose data has a measurement without a usable deviation had to leave the point out of the request, which changes the length of the request's residual vector and so its alignment with the residual vector and the jacobian of the problem the caller holds.

A positive infinity is now a standard deviation:

- The residual of the point is `+-0.0`, the difference over infinity, and its row of the jacobian is zeros, so it adds nothing to the cost and nothing to the gradient.
- The point stays in the residual vector, so the vector and the jacobian keep the length and the order of the problem's.
- The arithmetic needs no case of its own: the division the residual already is gives the zero.

Zero, a negative value, `NaN` and `-inf` are refused as before, and the message now says to pass a positive value or an infinity to drop the point.

For [sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim) this keeps the problems with such a measurement out of the python fallback: the refusal alone kept 53 of 81 real fit problems of the dependent model corpus out of the engine.
