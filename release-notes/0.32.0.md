# sbmlsim-core 0.32.0

A curve of a fit reads a value of the whole column of its condition, the largest, the smallest or the mean value, the column interpolated at times with the jumps of sbmlsim, or a parameter of the non-compartmental analysis of pkpdutils, so a fit is held to the data a study gives per simulation (#121, sbmlsim#625). The QFunction advances a point by as many step attempts per application as its caller allows, so a caller on a CPU applies it once per interval (#119).

## Features

- **The reduction of a curve** (#121, sbmlsim#625). A curve of `fit/1` carries an optional `reduction`, a value of the whole column of its condition which every measurement is compared with instead of the column at its rows: `max` and `min` (the largest or the smallest value), `mean` (the trapezoid mean over the time of the simulation), `at` (the column interpolated at the `times` of the curve with the jumps of sbmlsim, `grid_weights` ported operation for operation) and `pk` (a `parameter` of the non-compartmental analysis of pkpdutils, the kernel `pkpdutils-core` 0.7.0 which the python package runs as well, on the `batch` of one sample of `batch/1` whose time points and values the engine replaces with the rows of every simulation, the state before a change left out and the sample padded as sbmlsim pads it). `factor` and `time_shift` make the value the one of a host which converts a declared unit and reduces on a shifted axis. The jacobian of the sensitivities reduces the sensitivities of the column (the row of the extremum, the trapezoid mean, the weights of the interpolation); the one of `pk` is the central difference of the analysis along the sensitivity of every parameter, two analyses per parameter and no simulation. A curve of a reduction reads no `after_change` and no `noise_column`, and a request which carries them is refused. See [Fitting](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/fitting/#the-reduction-of-a-curve).
- **The kinds of a reduction a build fits** (#121). `sbmlsim_core.FIT_REDUCTIONS` names them (`max`, `min`, `mean`, `at`, `pk`). A release before 0.32.0 has no such constant and does not deny the field, so it would read the column at the rows of the times instead: a host reads the constant before it sends a reduction.
- **Step attempts per application of the QFunction** (#119). An application makes step attempts for a point until it is done or failed, at most `attempts` of the context, a whole number of at least 1 (before, one attempt per application). The sequence of steps of a point is the same whatever the attempts per application: 1, 3 and 1e6 give bitwise the same states, controls and counts. A caller on a CPU passes as many as a point may take and applies the QFunction once per interval, which reads and writes the fields of the points once per interval; one attempt per application keeps a call of bounded work. In slidesim's batched cells (41,252 cells of a liver model, 8 processes) the phase of the cells fell by about a tenth with one application per interval.

## Breaking

- The context of the QFunction has the new field `attempts`: `struct SbmlsimRosenbrockContext { double t1; double rtol; double atol; double h_min; double attempts; }` in `sbmlsim_rosenbrock.h`, `RosenbrockContext` of `sbmlsim-c` in Rust, so a caller which builds the context sets it; `attempts: 1.0` makes one attempt per application as before (#119).

## Unchanged

- The contract of a fit stays `fit/1`: a request without a `reduction` is fitted as before.
- A simulation, the WebAssembly target and the renderer.
