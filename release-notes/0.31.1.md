# sbmlsim-core 0.31.1

The legend columns, the monospace text and the signs of units which the notes of 0.31.0 name: their commit reached the branch of #116 after it was merged, so 0.31.0 was released without them (#115).

## Features

- **Legend columns** (#115). `Plot.legend_columns` (matplotlib's `ncol`) for every legend, inside a panel, right of a plot and of a row (a row legend takes the first in its row), filled top down; its `best` place and its room are those of the columns.
- **Monospace text** (#115). A text element takes `family: "monospace"`, measured at 0.6 of its size per character; the SVG names the monospace fonts of the systems, the PNG draws it with an embedded subset of Noto Sans Mono (Regular and Bold). Python `text_width(..., family="monospace")`.
- **The signs of units** (#115). The dot operator `⋅`, the arrows and the math operators Inter lacks are measured with the widths of Noto Sans Math and drawn in the PNG from an embedded subset of it; in monospace text `⋅` is written as `·`, which Noto Sans Mono has.

The fonts are OFL-1.1, as Inter (`crates/plot/fonts/LICENSE-Noto`, THIRD_PARTY.md); they add about 250 kB to the native binary, the wasm module is unchanged.
