# sbmlsim-core 0.40.0

PEtab v2 is fitted in a page: the optimizer of `sbmlsim-fit` runs in `compiler.wasm` and asks the engine of the page for its simulations (#157, fixes #147). The fit of a PEtab problem takes every prior, first-period values which are not linear, infinite bounds and the scales of sbmlsim's extension (#154, fixes #144). The reader of SBML flattens `comp`, which closes the last gaps of the core in the PEtab benchmark collection, now run in Rust with the pass rate of sbmlsim (#155, fixes #145). The live demo runs 0.39.0 and evaluates a PEtab problem in the page (#153, fixes #148).

## The fit of a PEtab problem in a page (#157)

```js
const fit = await problem.fit();                       // the fit of `sbmlsim-core petab --fit`
const best = await problem.fit({ starts: 8, seed: 1, maxIterations: 200, signal, onStep });
```

- **Module.** `problem.fit(options)` of `js/petab.mjs` fits the estimated parameters, the
  maximum likelihood estimate or, with priors, the maximum a posteriori estimate: `starts`
  (points or their number, drawn with `seed`), `algorithm` (`"trustRegion"`, the default,
  `"levenbergMarquardt"` or `{ differentialEvolution: {...} }`), `maxIterations`, the
  tolerances, `maxSeconds`, an `AbortSignal` which stops every fit at its next step with its
  best point, and `onStep`. The answer carries `x`, `objective`, `cost`, `iterations`,
  `evaluations`, `jacobians`, `status`, `simulations` and every fit as `runs`. A simulation
  which fails gives `{ error, kind }`, `kind` the one of the `EngineError`.
- **Ask/tell.** `compiler.wasm` exports `petab_fit_open`, `petab_fit_ask`, `petab_fit_tell`,
  `petab_fit_stop` and `petab_fit_close` (the feature `fit` of `sbmlsim-wasm`, on by default):
  one ask is every simulation a step needs (the residuals of a point, the `k + 1` evaluations
  of the finite differences of its jacobian, a generation of a differential evolution), which
  the page runs on the loader or on the workers of a pool next to each other.
- **`sbmlsim-fit` without the batch.** The feature `native` (the default) is the engine of
  `sbmlsim-host`, the batch, rayon, the contract `fit/1` and the identifiability; without it
  the crate builds for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`. `Opening::new` checks a definition,
  `Opening::simulations` are the requests at the start and `Opening::resolve` gives a
  `Problem` without a batch from the tables of a host; `Asking` drives the optimizers of
  basin ask/tell. A step which misses an evaluation is abandoned and run again, which repeats
  the arithmetic of the optimizer and no simulation.
- **The same fit.** On the native engine `Asking` gives the fit of `Problem::optimize` and
  `Executor::fit` to the bit (optimum, cost, iterations, evaluations, jacobians, status) for
  the trust region, Levenberg-Marquardt and the differential evolution with its polish, on the
  problem of the demo and the 49 cases of the PEtab test suite the fit carries. The page
  against the command line (`js/test/petab-fit.test.mjs`): 29 of the 49 cases identical to the
  bit, the objectives of the others within 7e-15 relative; the pool is the loader to the bit.
- **Hydrochlorothiazide.** The fit of the pharmacokinetics of hydrochlorothiazide of
  pkdb_models (6 parameters, 59 simulations, 777 measurements) finds the optimum of the
  command line (objective `-1631.5231473` against `-1631.5231468`, the parameters to `1e-6`):
  6.4 s in Node on the main thread, 2.1 s on a pool of 8 workers, 0.85 s on the command line.
- **No imports.** `crates/web-time`, a patch of `web-time` which re-exports `std::time`, keeps
  the clock of basin from pulling wasm-bindgen into `compiler.wasm`, which still imports
  nothing.
- **Size.** `compiler.wasm` grows from 3.42 MB to 3.83 MB (1.25 MB gzipped, +143 kB);
  `--no-default-features --features petab` builds it without the fit.

The jacobian of a fit in a page is the finite differences of the simulations, as the command
line takes by default: `engine.wasm` writes no sensitivities into its output (#156). The
button "fit k1 and k2" of the PEtab demo shows once the demo vendors this release.

See [JavaScript](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/javascript/#the-fit-of-a-problem)
and [Fitting](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/fitting/#a-fit-whose-host-simulates).

## The PEtab fit takes what its evaluation takes (#154)

- **Every prior.** `sbmlsim_fit::PriorKind` gains `cauchy`, `gamma`, `chisquare`,
  `exponential` and `rayleigh`, each with its log density and its derivative, so a prior row of
  the fit (and of the contract `fit/1`) takes all eleven kinds of PEtab v2. The offset of a
  prior row is twice the largest log density in the box, so the row is zero at the mode of the
  prior and Gauss-Newton steps to it.
- **First-period values which are not linear.** A derived value of a `Definition` carries an
  optional `form` (the trait `sbmlsim_fit::Form`: its value and its partial derivatives), added
  to the linear form, with the chain rule over its gradient in the jacobian of the
  sensitivities. PEtab passes the compiled formula, whose partials `Formula::derivative` takes
  with the operators of the analytic jacobian of the engine. The contract `fit/1` has no form.
- **Infinite bounds.** A bound may be `-inf` or `inf` (or an empty cell of the parameter
  table): the trust region and Levenberg-Marquardt search such a space as they search a box, a
  differential evolution refuses it with the parameter named. A log scale takes a lower bound
  of 0. PEtab searches a parameter within its bounds intersected with the support of its
  prior.
- **Scales of the extension.** `parameters.<id>.scale`, else `settings.parameter_scale`
  (`LINEAR`, `LOG`, `LOG10`) of sbmlsim's extension is the scale of the search, and an unknown
  name is refused; without the extension a parameter of positive finite bounds is searched on
  `log10`, any other on the linear scale.
- **An unbounded prior is refused.** A prior whose density grows without limit at a lower bound
  of 0 (gamma of a shape below 1, chi-squared of fewer than 2 degrees of freedom, log-Laplace of
  a scale above 1) gives a posterior without a maximum, and the fit refuses it with the kind of
  the prior and the parameter named ("the log-laplace prior of 'p_log_laplace' has a density
  which is not bounded"). That is `log-laplace(3, 5)` of the cases 0024 and 0025 of the
  PEtab test suite, whose evaluation still passes and whose fit passes with the parameter
  bounded at 1.
- **Improper flat priors.** The improper uniform prior of a parameter which has no prior and an
  infinite bound is left out of the objective of the fit (`cost_offset`).
- **Tests.** The fit of every case of the PEtab test suite is held to its negative log
  posterior; all eleven kinds against the reference values of scipy, their jacobians against
  central differences on the linear and the `log10` scale; fits with infinite bounds and with a
  lower bound of 0 on a log scale (`crates/fit/tests/unbounded.rs`); forms against central
  differences (`derived_jacobian.rs`).

See [PEtab](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/petab/#the-fit) and
[Fitting](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/fitting/#the-priors).

## comp in the reader, the PEtab benchmark collection in Rust (#155)

- **comp.** `sbmlsim_sbml::read` flattens a composed model into the model of SBML core it
  means, with the ids libSBML gives (`sub__x`, `outer__inner__x`): model definitions and
  external model definitions, deletions, replaced elements with their conversion factors,
  `replacedBy`, ports, `sBaseRef`s and the conversions of time and extent. `read_with` reads
  the documents of external model definitions through a function of the caller and
  `flatten_comp` gives the flattened document as text; `sbmlsim-core compile` and PEtab
  problems read them beside the model. A model with `comp` no longer needs
  `scripts/prepare_model.py`.
- **The comp cases of the SBML Test Suite.** `scripts/prepare_testsuite.py` writes the composed
  model of every case of `comp` (`comp/model.xml` with its external documents) and
  `sbmlsim-core testsuite --comp` runs the 125 cases from them: all pass, with every jacobian,
  without the optimizer and with the C target, which CI runs.
- **An `<apply>` of an `<apply>`.** An `<apply>` whose only child is an `<apply>` is that child,
  as libSBML reads it (`Beer_MolBioSystems2014`); an `<apply>` whose operator is an `<apply>`
  with arguments, a function of higher order, is refused.
- **Steady state after a single time.** The shortcut of the engine for a request at a single
  time skipped `steady_state_after`, so a measurement at `inf` without an experiment had the
  wrong value (`Blasi_CellSystems2016`). Fixed in the engine, with tests in the engine and in
  `crates/petab`.
- **Benchmark collection.** `crates/petab/tests/benchmark.rs` evaluates the problems sbmlsim
  downloads and converts (`uv run scripts/petab_benchmark.py download`, commit `c44d5ede`) at
  their nominal values and compares the simulations (1e-3 absolute and relative) and the
  log-likelihood with AMICI. 28 of 34 pass, the pass rate of sbmlsim in python, and the six
  which do not are the same six for the same reasons, listed in
  `crates/petab/tests/data/benchmark_baseline.json`; the test fails on a change in either
  direction and skips without the collection. CI caches the collection. The whole collection
  reads, compiles and evaluates in 1.3 s in a release build.

See [Compiling a model](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/compiling/),
[The SBML Test Suite](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/testsuite/) and
[PEtab](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/petab/#the-petab-benchmark-collection).

## Code generation (#158)

The symbols of the generated ODE code are deduplicated in linear time: the generator tracked
the ids it had given in a list it scanned for every new symbol, which made large exports
quadratic. The names and the files of every export stay the same.

## Behaviour changes

- `compiler.wasm` is built with the feature `fit` of `sbmlsim-wasm` by default and grows from
  3.42 MB to 3.83 MB (+143 kB gzipped) (#157).
- `sbmlsim-fit` has a feature `native`, on by default; a dependent which turns the default
  features off gets the crate without the batch, the contract `fit/1` and the identifiability
  (#157).
- A simulation of `problem.simulations()` which fails gives `{ error, kind }` instead of
  `{ error }` (#157).
- `sbmlsim-core petab --fit` prints its line at full precision (#157).
- The fit of a PEtab problem with priors has another `cost`: the offset of a prior row is twice
  the largest log density in the box instead of `max(2L, 0)`, and an improper flat prior is
  left out of `cost_offset`. Its optimum and its objective are those of PEtab (#154).
- The fit of a PEtab problem written by sbmlsim searches a parameter on the scale its extension
  names (`parameters.<id>.scale`, `settings.parameter_scale`) instead of the choice by its
  bounds, which a problem without the extension keeps (#154).
- A prior of the kinds `cauchy`, `chisquare`, `exponential`, `gamma` or `rayleigh`, a
  first-period value which is not linear and an infinite bound are fitted instead of refused;
  a prior which is not bounded at a lower bound of 0 is refused (#154).
- A point of a fit at a bound of a log scale stays on the bound (`10^log10(5)` was
  `5.000000000000001`) (#154).
- A document with `comp` is read and flattened instead of refused (#155).
