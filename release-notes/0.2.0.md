# Release notes for sbmlsim-core 0.2.0

A performance round of the integrator and of the code of the models: the PBPK models simulate 1.1 to 1.6 times faster than with 0.1.0 in Node and Chrome, the larger models with KLU 1.35 to 6.7 times. The method, the profile and every number are in `bench/RESULTS.md` ("Performance round").

## Breaking

- **The contract of a compiled model is `model/2`** (was `model/1`). A model compiled by 0.1.0 is refused with the error of its contract; compile it again. `model.json` has the new field `n_constants`, `model_sizes` writes seven words, `model_selections` takes a mask of the requested selections.

## Faster

- **Constants of the model.** The subexpressions of the math which read only parameters (and the assignment rules of such parameters: `pow(BW, 0.75)`, a body surface area) are computed once per change of the parameters (by `model_init` and `model_assign`) instead of in every call of the right hand side, the Jacobian, the root functions and the selections, with the bits of the code as written.
- **The output.** A row of the output no longer evaluates the assignment rules twice, and an expensive selection (the rate of a reaction) is computed only when the request selects it.
- **`pow`, `exp` and `log`** of `mathlib.wasm` are ports of ARM optimized-routines (the code of glibc): `pow` 3.5 times faster in wasm. 99.9 % of the results are bitwise those of glibc, the others within 1 ulp; the results of a simulation change in the last bits.
- **KLU** in the ordering AMD (Chen 6 times faster), its symbolic analysis kept across the restarts of CVODE at discontinuities and events, 32-bit indices in SUNDIALS and KLU. `KLU_THRESHOLD` is 12 (was 16).

On the benchmark no model takes more than 1.4 times the time of roadrunner native in wasmtime, Node or Chrome (was 1.6); the PBPK models take 0.57 to 0.83 of it, Chen with KLU 0.16 to 0.19. Every model agrees with roadrunner at tight tolerances, and the SBML Test Suite gives the results of 0.1.0 (1581 of 1789 cases pass).

## New

- `sbmlsim-core bench --host native` measures the engine compiled for the machine (`Runtime::instantiate_native`), the bound of the integrator without WebAssembly; the benchmark reports roadrunner, native, wasmtime, Node and Chrome.

## Not changed

Linear algebra libraries of the kind of LAPACK and BLAS were measured and not taken: the Newton matrices of the models are small or very sparse, and KLU is 2 to 250 times faster than a dense LU (`bench/RESULTS.md`, "Linear algebra").
