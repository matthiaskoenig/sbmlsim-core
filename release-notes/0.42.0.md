# sbmlsim-core 0.42.0

The statistics of the analyses of sbmlsim run in Rust: the new crate `sbmlsim-stats`, in python `sbmlsim_core.stats`, holds the special functions, the linear algebra and the kernels of the evaluation of a fit, of its Fisher information and posterior, of the uncertainty and of the visual predictive check, which sbmlsim computed with numpy and scipy (#166). sbmlsim takes them in a release of its own; nothing of the simulations, the fits or the PEtab problems changes.

## The statistics (#166)

```python
from sbmlsim_core import stats

fisher = stats.fisher_statistics(stats.gram(jacobian), cost=0.05, n=20)
u = stats.unit_points(2, 1024, design="sobol", seed=1)
z = stats.copula(u, [[1.0, 0.8], [0.8, 1.0]])
print(stats.quantile_ci(z[:, 0] + z[:, 1], 0.95))
```

- **The kernels.** The metrics of a fit and the criteria of its data points
  (`sbmlsim.fit.metrics`, `sbmlsim.fit.evaluation`); the Fisher information of a jacobian and
  its statistics, intervals and relative errors (`sbmlsim.fit.fisher`); the Laplace posterior
  and its correlations (`sbmlsim.fit.posterior`); the statistics of the draws, their
  quantiles and intervals, and the parameters of a reported interval
  (`sbmlsim.uncertainty.statistics`); the points of the unit cube (`"sobol"`, `"lhs"`,
  `"random"`), the Gaussian copula and the nearest correlation matrix
  (`sbmlsim.uncertainty.draws`, `.covariance`); the anytime-valid p-values, the confidence
  sequences and the decisions of the probability statements (`sbmlsim.uncertainty.testing`);
  the group means and bands of the predictive check (`sbmlsim.fit.variability`). Underneath:
  the normal, t, binomial and incomplete beta distributions and the eigendecomposition
  (cyclic Jacobi), the factors and the pseudo-inverse of up to 200 parameters.
- **The python's behaviour.** Every function has the name, the edges and the messages of the
  python it replaces: an argument the python refuses raises its exception with its message (a
  `ValueError`, an `OverflowError`, a `ZeroDivisionError` or an `IndexError`), and `nan` comes
  back where the python returns `nan`. The class of an exception is the `ErrorKind` of the
  error, set where the error is made.
- **Accuracy.** Fixtures computed once with numpy, scipy and sbmlsim hold the deterministic
  kernels to `1e-12` relative, the normal distribution to `1e-13`, the incomplete beta, t and
  binomial distributions to `1e-10` and the linear algebra to `1e-10` of the largest value of
  the matrix. Where the crate and scipy differ beyond the rounding, the crate is the more
  accurate: its logarithm of the beta function keeps the digits `scipy.special.betaln` loses
  at a large and a small parameter.
- **Determinism.** libm and xoshiro256++ give the same bits on every target; the hash tests run
  natively and under wasmtime. The crate builds for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`; its JavaScript
  binding follows.
- **Sizes.** A size is counted without a wrap, also with a `usize` of 32 bits: an array past
  what an array holds raises numpy's `ValueError` (`array is too big`) before any memory is
  asked for. The random points continue from any `start` in a time logarithmic in it, by an
  exact jump-ahead of the stream (`Stream::advance` of `sbmlsim-sensitivity`, which leaves its
  draws unchanged). Every function on arrays releases the GIL while it computes.

See [Statistics](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/stats/).

## Behaviour changes

- None for the existing functions. The draws of `sbmlsim_core.stats` are deliberately not the
  streams of scipy and numpy (the Sobol' sequence of sbmlsim-core with LMS and a digital
  shift, xoshiro256++ for the random points, the Latin hypercube and the resampling): once
  sbmlsim draws through them, a seed gives other points than before.
