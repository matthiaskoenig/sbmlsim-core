# sbmlsim-core 0.27.0

The engine draws figures. A new crate, `sbmlsim-plot`, renders one JSON description of a figure to SVG and PNG, natively from python and inside a page through `plot.wasm`, so a figure of a report and the figure of an interactive panel are one drawing.

## Features

- **A renderer of figures** (#97). The crate `sbmlsim-plot` draws the contract `figure/1`: plots of curves, bands, areas, rules, text, bars, boxes, violins, histograms, heatmaps and polygons with linear, log and category axes, colour scales, a grid of plots with titles, legends inside and outside and a right axis. SVG and PNG are written natively, with the Inter fonts bundled, so a figure looks the same on every machine, and the SVG of the native renderer and of `plot.wasm` are the same bytes. The documentation has a new page, Figures, with the contract and its limits.
  - Python: `FIGURE_CONTRACT`, `render_svg`, `render_png`, `figure_geometry`, `pick`, `color_scale` and `plot_wasm`. `figure_geometry` gives the size of the figure and where its panels, their scales and the legend entries are in pixels, and `pick` the data point under a pointer.
  - JavaScript: `loadPlot` loads `plot.wasm`, and a `PreparedFigure` prepares a figure once, so a pointer moving over it asks for the geometry and the picks without laying the figure out again.
  - Limits: a figure is at most 16384 px wide or high, a grid at most 64 by 64 plots, and a PNG at most 64 Mpx.
  - Measured: the benchmark figure renders in about 1 to 1.5 ms natively, a pick on a prepared figure takes about 0.1 ms, and a PNG at 144 dpi about 36 ms. `plot.wasm` is 1.32 MB, 394 kB gzipped.
