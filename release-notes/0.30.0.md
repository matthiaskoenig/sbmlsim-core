# sbmlsim-core 0.30.0

A model runs without WebAssembly: as native C for the engine, advanced one interval at a time from a state its caller gives, or as a libCEED QFunction with a Rosenbrock integrator which advances the cells of a whole tissue together. The renderer draws what the custom matplotlib figures of pkdb_models and pkpdsim need (#107, #108), and a legend inside stays inside where matplotlib would put it.

## Features

- **The C target** (#111). The new crate `sbmlsim-c` translates SBML into C (`generate`): the functions of `model/2` from the programs of the optimizer, as `model.wasm` has them, each a pure function of `t`, `y`, `p` and its outputs under the prefix `sbmlsim_model_<id>_`, with the math of `<math.h>`. `Library::build` compiles the C with `$CC` or `cc` (`-O3 -march=native -ffp-contract=off -fno-fast-math`) into a cache keyed by the flags, the compiler, its predefined macros and the source (`$SBMLSIM_C_CACHE`, else `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/sbmlsim/c` or `~/.cache/sbmlsim/c`), and `CModel` is the library as the `ModelFns` of the engine. The right hand side and the Jacobian agree with `model.wasm` within 1e-13 relative, and the native simulation is 2.5 times faster than `engine.wasm` in wasmtime. `sbmlsim-core testsuite --model-code c` (feature `native`) runs the SBML Test Suite on it: every case of the baseline passes but the 53 models which read their history (`delay`), which it refuses, pinned in `testdata/testsuite_refused_c.json`.
- **A stepper** (#111). `sbmlsim_engine::Stepper` is an instance of a model which is advanced from a given state one interval at a time, for a caller which couples the model to something else and changes its state in between (operator splitting). `Stepper::new` initializes the model as a simulation does, with changes as pre-initialization values; `advance(y, t0, t1)` integrates as a simulation does, with the events and the discontinuities of the interval, the state of the events carried from one advance to the next. `state()`, `parameters()` and `memory_bytes()` (19 kB for the cell model of `testdata/models`, within 1% of its resident memory).
- **The QFunction target** (#111). `sbmlsim_c::generate_qfunction` writes a model with the Rosenbrock integrator RODAS4 (order 4, an embedded solution of order 3, both L-stable, one dense LU with partial pivoting per step) as one libCEED QFunction, plain C99 for the CPU backends and the JIT of the GPU backends: one step attempt per point and application, so the steps of a point depend on no other point, on no blocking and on no backend. `run_points` runs it as libCEED calls it, without libCEED. On the cell model of `testdata/models` it stays within `2e-6 |y| + 2e-9` of the stepper at tight tolerances, and advances 167000 cells by 60 min per second on one core against 88000 for a stepper per cell at the same tolerances. Models with events, delays or algebraic rules are refused.
- **Renderer fields of `figure/1`** (#113, closes #107 and #108), each optional and left out of a spec without it: `line_visible` of an axis hides its line; `aspect` of a plot (`equal` or a positive number) widens the limits of one axis so a unit of y is as long as a unit of x (matplotlib's `adjustable="datalim"`); `error_cap` of a curve or a bar draws the caps of its error bars; `x_edges` and `y_edges` of a heatmap put its cells between edges on numeric axes (`pcolormesh`), with a pick of the edges; `size` and `weight` of a text.
- **Colour maps of matplotlib** (#113). `ColorScale.name` takes every sequential and diverging map of matplotlib in lower case and its reversal `<name>_r` (108 names, `sbmlsim_core.COLOR_SCALES`), with the colours of matplotlib 3.11.2; a name in the case of matplotlib (`Blues`) is refused with a hint.
- **The location of a legend** (#113). `loc` of the legend settings and `legend_loc` of a plot place a legend inside at a location of matplotlib's `loc` (`upper_right`, `upper_left`, `lower_left`, `lower_right`, `right`, `center_left`, `center_right`, `lower_center`, `upper_center`, `center`), or at `best`, the default: of matplotlib's nine locations the one whose frame covers the least ink of the data, in matplotlib's order on a tie.

## Behaviour changes of the renderer

- A legend inside stays inside however much data it covers, as in matplotlib, and goes right of its plot only when it does not fit into its panel; before, only the four corners were candidates and a legend covering more than 5 % of its frame with data went outside (#113).
- The axis lines, ticks, tick labels and the neutral legend swatch are `#3b424d`, darker than `#5b6472` before (#113).

## Fixes

- The least panel of 20 pixels holds for the span of a plot, not for every column and row of the grid, so a grid of many thin columns or rows no longer draws its last plots beyond the edge of the figure; a grid which cannot give its plots that panel grows the figure, as a legend outside does (#113).
- A text keeps its runs of spaces and the spaces at its ends: they are written as no-break spaces, which no renderer collapses (#113).

- A steady state after the end of a model without events starts from the state the output ended in, no longer from the start of the last segment (`x' = exp(-t)` got 1 instead of `2 - 1/e`).

## Unchanged

- A simulation, the bindings and the WebAssembly target: the C and the QFunction target are new crates and functions beside them, and the integration a stepper runs is the one of a simulation, which the SBML Test Suite passes against its baseline.
- A spec of 0.29 parses as before; it draws as before but for the colour of the axes and the place of a legend inside, see above.
