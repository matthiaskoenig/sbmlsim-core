# Release notes for sbmlsim-core 0.6.0

A change of a simulation adds to a value as well as it sets one: a repeated dose is an increment of its dose in the state before it, and the panel endpoint gives the dosing protocols of a panel of sbmlsim (a dose repeated once, twice or three times daily) as such changes. The results of a simulation are the ones of 0.5.0.

## New

- **The increments of a change** (`simulation/1`). A change has the optional `increments`, `[[id, increment], ...]`, each added to the value of its id in the state before the change (an amount `S`, a concentration `[S]`, the value of any other entity), at once with its `values`: the formula `"PODOSE_x + 25"` of a change of sbmlsim. One change sets or increments an id once; an increment which is not finite is refused. A request without increments is the one of 0.5.0, and an engine before 0.6.0 refuses one with increments as an unknown field.
- **The dosing protocols of a panel** (`panels.mjs`, the bundle `panelbundle/1` of sbmlsim 0.28). The `dosing` of the run of a panel adds the dose of its knob to its target again at the times `k * interval` before the duration and the end (`doseTimes`, `MAX_DOSES`, as `dose_times` of sbmlsim), as increments of the changes of the request of every point. A dose at an output time is two rows of the engine, the state before it and after it; the curve has the state after it, as the service of sbmlsim interpolates it.
