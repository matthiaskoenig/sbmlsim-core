# sbmlsim-core 0.31.0

The renderer draws the scan figures of pkdb_models as matplotlib draws them: colour bars of colour maps given by stops, with marks at the default and above a row or inside a panel, fixed ticks, the markers of matplotlib, legends with titles and columns, and the signs of units in every text (#115, #116).

## Features

- **Colour scales from stops** (#116). `ColorScale.stops`, a list of positions in [0, 1] with `#rrggbb` colours, interpolated as matplotlib's `LinearSegmentedColormap.from_list`, two stops at one position a step; a scale has a `name` or `stops`. Python `color_scale` takes `stops` in place of the name.
- **Colour bars** (#116): `marks`, lines across the bar at values (black, 2 px by default); `placement` `right` (as before), `top` (horizontal, centred above the panels of its row, one per row) or `inset` (horizontal inside its panel at a legend location, which a `best` legend avoids); `length` (matplotlib's `shrink`) and `thickness`.
- **Ticks of an axis** (#116). `Axis.ticks` and `tick_labels` fix the ticks; a tick outside the limits is dropped and does not widen them.
- **Tick labels** (#116). A `\n` breaks a tick label of an axis or a colour bar into lines, `^{...}` is a superscript, the decades of a log axis are written 10ⁿ, and a linear axis whose largest tick is at least 1e6 or below 1e-4 writes mantissas with a ×10ⁿ multiplier at its end, as matplotlib.
- **Markers** (#116): `triangle_down`, `triangle_left`, `triangle_right`, `star`, `x_filled`, `plus_filled`, `pentagon`, `hexagon` and `thin_diamond`, and the half fills of matplotlib (`fill_style` `left`, `right`, `bottom`, `top` with `fill_alt`).
- **Boxes and error bars** (#116): the `width` of a box and the `error_width` of the error bars of a curve or a bar.
- **Legends** (#116): `Plot.legend_title`; `Plot.legend_columns` (matplotlib's `ncol`) of every legend, inside a panel, right of a plot and of a row, filled top down; the legend `position` `bottom`, below the grid, with `columns` and `title`; a legend group of a band and a curve shows the band behind the line.
- **Fonts** (#116): `Fonts.plot_title`, the size of the plot titles apart from the figure title.
- **Monospace text** (#116). A text element takes `family: "monospace"`, measured at 0.6 of its size per character; the PNG draws it with an embedded subset of Noto Sans Mono.
- **The signs of units** (#116). The dot operator `⋅`, the arrows and the math operators which Inter lacks are measured and, in the PNG, drawn from an embedded subset of Noto Sans Math.

## Behaviour changes of the renderer

- The markers have the shapes of matplotlib: `diamond` is matplotlib's `D` (a square on its corner, before 0.75 as wide as high, now `thin_diamond`), a square's side and a triangle's width and height are its size (#116).
- The tick labels of a log axis are the decades as 10ⁿ and those of a linear axis of large or small values carry a multiplier (#116).

## Unchanged

- A spec of 0.30 parses as before; every new field is optional.
