# sbmlsim-core 0.45.0

The units of sbmlsim, the registry of pint, run in Rust: the crate `sbmlsim-units`, with its python module `sbmlsim_core.units` and its JavaScript module `sbmlsim-core/units` on `compiler.wasm`. The planner of a scan of sbmlsim runs in Rust as well: the crate `sbmlsim-scan` plans a scan into the requests of the engine, evaluates its observables and assembles its result, in python (`sbmlsim_core.scan`) and in a page (`sbmlsim-core/scan`), and gives the plans of a scan (`plan/1`) to a host which simulates them itself. `model/2` carries the units sbmlsim gives the entities of a model, so a host gets them from the compiled model alone.

## The units crate

```python
from sbmlsim_core import units

registry = units.Registry()
registry.factor("mg/min", "g/hr")  # 0.06
registry.parse("mg/l")  # (0.9999999999999999, {"[length]": -3.0, "[mass]": 1.0})
registry.define("IU_insulin", "0.0347 * mg")
model = registry.with_model_units(unit_ids={"mg_per_min": "mg/min"})
model.factor("mg_per_min/kg", "mg/min/kg")  # 1.0
```

- **One authority.** `sbmlsim-units` parses the unit expressions sbmlsim uses (products, quotients, integer powers, parentheses, numbers such as `ml/min/(1.73*m^2)`, the prefixes of SI with `µ`, `μ` and `u`, the short and long names of pint and their plurals, `M` for molar, and `none`, `item` and `percent` of sbmlsim) into a factor to the root units of pint and the exponents of their dimensions, and converts between two units with the factor of their quotient, as pint converts. The scan planner, python and JavaScript convert with it.
- **The numbers of pint.** The factor is computed down the definitions of pint (`hour = 60 * minute`, `liter = decimeter ** 3`) with its cancellation of equal scales and its order of the powers (the `pow` of `libm`, the same bits natively and in wasm): every one of the 177 expressions of the corpus of sbmlsim (`testdata/units_corpus.json`) has pint's factor to the last bit, and the parity test holds it to 1e-15 relative in Rust, python and JavaScript. `l` to `ml` is exactly 1000.
- **Definitions and models.** `define(name, expression)` defines a unit (`IU_insulin = 0.0347 * mg`) and refuses a redefinition with another value; `with_model_units(units, unit_ids)` is a registry with the unit definitions of a model as units of its expressions (`mg_per_min/kg`), a unit id replaced as a whole and winning over a unit of the same name, as `UnitsInformation.quantity` of sbmlsim does, and the unit expressions of its entities (`unit_of`).
- **Errors.** `UnitError` names the expressions: two units of other dimensions name both (`cannot convert 'mg' ([mass]) to 'ml' ([length] ** 3)`), a temperature with an offset (`degC`, `degF`) is known and refused naming the unit, a power which is no integer is refused. In python it is `sbmlsim_core.UnitError`, a `SbmlsimCoreError`.
- **Bindings.** Python: `sbmlsim_core.units.Registry` with `parse`, `factor`, `convert`, `define`, `with_model_units` and `unit_of`. `compiler.wasm` exports `units_open`, `units_define`, `units_parse`, `units_factor`, `units_with_model_units`, `units_unit_of` and `units_close` (the feature `units` of `sbmlsim-wasm`, on by default, 56 kB, 20 kB gzipped), which `js/units.mjs` drives: `createUnits(compiler)` gives `parse`, `factor`, `convert`, `define`, `withModelUnits`, `unitOf` and `close`. See [Units](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/units/).

## The planner of a scan

The crate `sbmlsim-scan` plans a scan of sbmlsim, a simulation and its dimensions (the request `scan/1`), into the requests `simulation/1` of the engine, from the description of the compiled model alone, so a host of python or JavaScript runs a scan of sbmlsim without sbmlsim's planner.

- **Ask and tell.** `Scan::open(request, description)` checks the request (unknown fields refused, a target the model does not have named), `ask` gives the next round of requests, at most 250 points of one simulation in the C order of the scan as sbmlsim's engine core cuts them, `tell` takes the output or the error of every request and `result` is the result of the scan. It simulates nothing itself and builds for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`.
- **The requests of sbmlsim, bitwise.** The times and values converted into the units of the model with `sbmlsim-units`, the output times merged within the resolution of the time, the values of the dimensions applied as `Plan.with_values` applies them (also at a time, `at`), the formulas of the pre-initialization folded, the steady states before the start and after the end, a time shift, a negative start, a grid of times and a block of points: the requests of the 15 oracle cases of sbmlsim without folds, events or observables (`testdata/scan`) are the ones sbmlsim sends, every number by its bits.
- **Folds and events.** A formula at a change which reads the state of the model (`[C] * [C] / 4`, also a linear one such as `Vmax_bA + 1`, which a run of sbmlsim folds as well) is folded into the number of the change by simulating the segment which ends at the change: a chunk asks one round per such change of its longest protocol, the k-th segment of every point which still folds as one batch, each segment resuming from the state after the change before it, and then the round of its points, as sbmlsim's engine core batches them. The segment of a model with events ends at the state before the events of the time of the change (`before_events`). A segment which fails fails its point alone (flagged, or raised with `on_error: raise` once the points of the chunk ran). The rounds of the 5 oracle cases of folds, events and the steady state after the end are the ones sbmlsim sends, every number by its bits, with the engine answering every round.
- **Observables.** The observables of sbmlsim (`Formula`, `PK` and `Custom`, the `to_dict` of each) are evaluated on the native output of every point, before any interpolation, as sbmlsim's `compile_observables` orders, checks and prunes them to `keep`: a `Formula` with the math of PEtab through the operations of the engine (`sbmlsim_petab::math`), with the reductions `max`, `min`, `mean` and `at` over the time of the point and names with a dot (`pk.cmax`), its kind and its unit derived with the algebra of pint (`mmol/l / (mol/m^3)` is `meter ** 3 * millimole / liter / mole`) and converted into a declared unit; a `PK` observable with the kernel of pkpdutils (`pkpdutils-core` 0.7.0, the tag of `sbmlsim-fit`), its doses read from the plan of every point, its parameters found by the probe of sbmlsim and the points of a chunk analysed in groups of the same number of doses and the same padded length; a `Custom` observable, a function of the host, in a round of its own (`Ask::Observe` with the finite time points of every point, the columns the functions read and the symbols among them which are a value per simulation, answered by `tell_observed`, whose `errors` fail a point). The requests of a run select what the observables read. A point fails exactly when its own simulation or its own observables fail.
- **The result `scanresult/1`.** `result` assembles the arrays of sbmlsim's `ScanResult`, which a host rebuilds into its dataset: the dimensions with their sizes, the coordinates in the order of the dataset (the labels of every dimension, the values of its targets as given, under `<dimension>.<target>` where a variable has the name, its covariates, `_index` of a block, `time` of a grid) with their units, the variables (the time of a ragged result, the kept timecourses, the values per simulation, `status`) with their dimensions, shape, unit and values flat in C order, the units in the order of the dataset, the times of the changes, the first 10 failed points of `on_error: flag` and the block of a run of `points`. The timecourses are on the grid of `time` (interpolated with the rules of sbmlsim's `grid_weights`, operation for operation), on the output times every simulation shares, on the union of output times which differ only at their changes, or ragged over `_point` padded with `NaN`. The results of the 24 oracle cases of sbmlsim and the error of the raising one are sbmlsim's, every value by its bits, observables and interpolated values included.
- **The rules of the timecourses, once.** The interpolation onto a grid with its jumps at changes and events (`grid_weights`), the samples of a PK analysis without the states before the changes, their padded length and the reductions `max`, `min` and `mean` are the new crate `sbmlsim-timecourse`, which the fit (its reductions of `fit/1`) and the planner both call, so the two agree by construction; the fit computes the numbers it computed.
- **Non-finite numbers.** As JSON a number of an array of `scanresult/1` is a number, `NaN` is `null` and the infinities are `"inf"` and `"-inf"`, as the fixtures of sbmlsim write them (`"NaN"` is read as well), so a serialized result reads back as itself, every value by its bits. A point whose output times are not the ones of the grid the simulations share fails; it is neither padded nor cut.
- **Values which are not finite.** A value of a change written as `"inf"`, `"-inf"` or `"NaN"` (as sbmlsim writes a number which is not finite, and as a request is written back) is that number and no formula, so a change to infinity sets the number, as sbmlsim does, and a request read back reads as itself.
- **Large scans.** The planner checks that the labels of a dimension are unique and gives the output times of a simulation once each in a time linear in their number: a dimension of 20000 points is checked in a few milliseconds.
- **Units.** `sbmlsim-units` names a unit as pint does (`Registry::named`, `Named`: `mmol/l` is `millimole / liter`, products, quotients and powers without converting one unit into another, pint's default format with its sorted names), which the units of the observables are written with.
- **Dimensions of models.** A scan with a dimension of models (sbmlsim's `Dimension(models=...)`) is planned against the compiled model of every key of the dimension, which replaces the model of the run as in sbmlsim's `Simulator.run(None, scan)`: `Scan::open_with_models(request, None, models)` takes the description of every model by its key, plans a simulation per model (the plans of every simulation and model, the values of the dimensions in the units of every model), keeps the points in the C order of the scan, also for a block of `points` across the models, and `Ask::Simulate { model, requests }` names the model of the requests of a round (`None` for the model of the run). The models are checked against the first one as sbmlsim's `_check_model` does (every selection of the requests and every dose target of a PK observable, with the same unit), the result has the units of the first model, and a message names a model by its label (`The dimension 'd' does not fit the model 'other': ...`). The request carries what differs by model: `model_settings`, the settings of the requests of a model by its key (the absolute tolerance of every state), and `model_changes`, the changes of a model (sbmlsim's `AbstractModel.changes`), the defaults of the pre-initialization of its simulations (`SimulationDefinition::with_preinit_defaults`); `changes` are the changes of the model of the run, applied alike.
- **The checks of sbmlsim.** A coordinate never hides and is never hidden by another name of the result, sbmlsim's `_check_coordinates`: a covariate of the name of an output, a dimension id, a changed target, a covariate of another dimension or a name of the result (`time`, `status`, `_point`, `_index`, `statistic`), and a dimension of the name `<dimension>.<target>` of the values of a changed target which is an output, are errors with sbmlsim's messages. The checks of sbmlsim's `Dimension` and of its `Scan` have sbmlsim's messages as well (a dimension of several kinds, labels which do not fit or repeat, ids which appear twice, are names of the result or changed targets).
- **The first failed point.** With `on_error: raise` a scan of several plans (a dimension of simulations or of models) fails with the first failed point in the C order of the scan, as sbmlsim's `_run_serial` raises it: the chunks which start before a failed point still run, since one of another plan may hold a point before it which fails as well.
- **Messages.** A message lists names as python's `repr` lists them (`['status']`), and a grid of times of a unit is refused as sbmlsim's `time_magnitudes` refuses it (`The model has no unit of time, give the times as numbers, not in 'second'.`, `The times in 'milligram' cannot be converted to the unit of time 'second' of the model.`).
- **API.** `Scan` (`open`, `open_with_models`, `ask`, `tell`, `tell_observed`, `progress`, `finished`, `finished_models`, `result`), `Ask` (`Simulate { model, requests }`, `Observe`, `Done`), `ScanRequest` (with `changes`, the changes of the model of the run, `model_settings` and `model_changes`), `ScanResult` (`scanresult/1`), `HostObservables`, the question of `Ask::Observe` with the `scalars`, the columns which are a value per simulation, `HostObserved` with the `errors` of the points, `HostError`, `run_selections` (the selections and `keep` of a run without observables) and `Expanded`, the plans of every simulation and model (`model_axis`, `n_models`, `plan_of`, `model_of`, `model_of_plan`, `point_plan`).

## The plans of a scan

`compile_plans` of `sbmlsim-scan` and `sbmlsim_core.scan.compile_plans` give the plans of a scan, the contract `plan/1`, instead of running it: every simulation of the scan compiled against every model, the plan of each (the times in the time unit of the model, the quantities in the units of their targets, the kinds of the targets, the changes merged into one event per time, the output times, the steady states), the values of the dimensions in the units of the model and the times of the dimensions with `at`, so a host which simulates the plans itself runs the plans of the planner. sbmlsim's executors of roadrunner, COPASI and AMICI and its fit run these plans since its python compiler of plans was removed. A model is a `CompiledModel`, a `LoadedModel` or the description `model/2`; of a request `scan/1` only `scan`, `changes` and `model_changes` decide the plans. The plans of the 25 oracle cases are the plans the planner runs, every number by its bits, and the JSON reads back by its bits (`NaN` `null`, the infinities `"inf"` and `"-inf"`). `PLAN_CONTRACT` is the contract. See [Scans](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/scan/).

## Scans in python and in a page

The planner runs in python, `sbmlsim_core.scan`, and in a page, `sbmlsim-core/scan` on `compiler.wasm`: every oracle case of sbmlsim gives sbmlsim's result end to end in either, every value by its bits in python and every value but one parameter of a PK observable (1 unit in the last place, the kernel of pkpdutils in `compiler.wasm`) in a page, and the case which raises gives its error with sbmlsim's message. See [Scans](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/scan/).

```python
from sbmlsim_core import scan

result = scan.run(request, model, threads=4, observe=observe, on_batch=progress)
# an array of numpy of float64 in the shape of the variable
result["variables"][0]["values"]
```

- **Python.** `scan.run(request, model, *, models, threads, observe, on_batch)` drives the rounds on `model.batch_executor(threads)` without a request becoming an object of python, so the result does not depend on `threads`; `observe(question)` computes the observables of the host (`Custom`) of a round and `on_batch(done, total)` reports the points which ran. `scan.Scan(request, model, *, models)` is the planner for a host which simulates itself (`ask`, `tell`, `tell_observed`, `progress`, `result`, and `round_model`, the key of the model of the last round of simulations). A scan with a dimension of models takes the loaded model of every key of the dimension, `scan.run(request, None, models={"fast": fast, "slow": slow})`, whose requests run on a batch executor of their model, opened at the first round of the model and closed once every point of it ran (`finished_models()`). A request is a dict written with `json.dumps` and read from that text, so its mappings keep their order; the arrays of the result are arrays of numpy of float64 in the shape of their variable, built without JSON. `sbmlsim_core.ScanError` (a `SbmlsimCoreError`) has the `kind` (`request`, `units`, `unsupported`, `point`) and the `point` of the failure; `SCAN_CONTRACT` and `SCAN_RESULT_CONTRACT` are the contracts.
- **JavaScript.** `runScan(request, { compiler, runtime | pool, model, models, observe, onBatch, signal, poolWindow })` gives the result with `Float64Array`s, the workers of a pool changing no bit; `models` is the compiled model of every key of a dimension of models (`{ fast, slow }`), each simulated on an engine of its own, made at its first round and dropped (its model removed from a pool) once every point of it ran; `openScan(request, { compiler, model, models })` is the planner for a host which simulates itself, whose round of simulations names its model (`{ simulate, model }`); `ScanError` has `kind` and `point`. `compiler.wasm` exports `scan_open` (the request, the description of the model of the run and the descriptions of the models of a dimension of models by their keys), `scan_ask`, `scan_tell`, `scan_observed`, `scan_result` and `scan_close` (the feature `scan` of `sbmlsim-wasm`, on by default), every array of numbers as the little-endian bytes of its doubles next to the JSON.
- **The planner.** `Scan::progress` gives the points which ran and the points of the block, and `Scan::finished` whether `result` assembles a result, so a host does not give up a scan which is not done.
- **The build.** `compiler.wasm`, which `sbmlsim-host` embeds, is built again when `sbmlsim-scan` or `sbmlsim-timecourse` changes.

## The description of a model

`model/2` gains four fields, absent by default, so a description of a release before reads as it did (a reader before 0.45.0 refuses them as unknown fields): `time_unit`, the unit of the time of the model, `initial_assignments`, the parameters, compartments and species references whose value as loaded an initial assignment computes, `time_dependent`, whether a math of the model reads the time or a delay or an event has a delay, and `unit_definitions`, the unit definitions the units name by their id because pint has no unit of them, with their expressions of pint. The planner of a scan reads them and refuses a description without `time_unit`, which a compiler before 0.45.0 wrote, asking to compile the model again.

- **The units of sbmlsim.** `units` and `time_unit` are the units sbmlsim gives the entities of the model, `UnitsInformation.from_sbml_doc` of sbmlsim ported into the compiler, so a host gets the units of a result from the compiled model alone: `time` (`second` without a time unit), every compartment, species (`S` and `[S]`) and parameter, an entity without units empty (the default units of the model name none, as in sbmlsim), and every reaction with a kinetic law, the unit libsbml 5.21.2 derives for its kinetic law (`UnitFormulaFormatter`, ported with what decides a unit: a sum of units which are not equivalent has none, a number without units is undeclared, a product of more than two terms simplifies as binary ones nested to the left, the multiplier of a cancelled kind goes to the first unit left) named by the first unit definition of the same unit or else written by sbmlsim's rules. A unit definition is written as pint reads it (`mmol/min`, `1/min`, `mmol/l`, `kg`, `cm^2`), one with a factor which is no prefix named by its id (`mmHg`, or `hr_2` where pint has `hr` as another unit) and carried with its expression in `unit_definitions`, so a registry converts into it (`with_model_units`). They were the factor expressions of the compiler (`0.001*mole*(60*second)**-1`, `extent/time` for a reaction). The units of the 24 models of sbmlsim (`testdata/model_units`, 1615 entities, among them a model of the rules of libsbml) are sbmlsim's byte for byte, and so are 18706 of the 18707 units of the 1692 models of the SBML Test Suite of Level 3 Version 2; the one which is not (`00856`, a call of a function definition in the arguments of a call of it) depends on a cache of libsbml keyed by the addresses of freed nodes, which no port reproduces.

## Fixes

- A species of an SBML of Level 1 or 2 without substance units has the built in `substance`, and a compartment without units the built in unit of its dimensions (`volume`, `area`, `length`), as the conversion of libsbml to Level 3 states them, so their units in `model/2` are the ones sbmlsim gives them (`item` and `fl` of the repressilator, whose `substance` and `volume` are redefined).
- A compartment of an SBML of Level 1 or 2 without `spatialDimensions` has three dimensions, as these levels define, so its unit and the unit of the concentration of its species are the ones of a volume (they were missing).
