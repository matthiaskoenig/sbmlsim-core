# Release notes for sbmlsim-core 0.15.0

A least squares run of a fit takes the tolerance of its step and the tolerance of its cost, the `xtol` and the `ftol` of `scipy.optimize.least_squares`. A run without them has no test of either and spends its last iterations on the noise of the integrator: on the fits of the HCTZ model of sbmlsim that is 2.3 times the evaluations of a run which stops at `1e-8` and finds the same optimum.

## New

- **`step_tolerance` and `cost_tolerance` of the optimizer of a fit** ([#57](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim-core/pull/57), for [sbmlsim#607](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/607)). Two more settings of `optimizer` in a request of `fit/1`, next to `gradient_tolerance`:

    ```python
    request["optimizer"] = {
        "algorithm": "trust_region",
        "step_tolerance": 1e-8,  # the `xtol` of scipy: the step, relative to the point
        "cost_tolerance": 1e-8,  # the `ftol` of scipy: the change of the cost, relative to it
    }
    answer = model.fit(request)
    ```

    Both are absent by default, which is the behaviour of the releases before this one: the optimizers of [basin](https://docs.rs/basin) have no test of the step and no test of the cost unless one is asked for, and only the gradient carries a default of its own. A run which converges at one of them says so in its status, `RelativeParamTolerance` or `RelativeCostTolerance`, where a run without them reaches `NumericalNoProgress` or `SolverFailed` after its steps have stopped moving the cost by more than the noise of the integration.

    A caller knows the tolerance of its integrator and the cost of a fit is exact only to about that size, so this is the tolerance to pass: a simulation at a relative tolerance of `1e-8` makes every step below `1e-8` a step into noise. The two settings reach `with_relative_step_tolerance` and `with_relative_cost_change_tolerance` of the solver, so they hold for `trust_region` and for `levenberg_marquardt` alike, and the polish of a `differential_evolution` inherits them with the rest of its settings.

    They move no optimum: on the fits of the decay model a run with both at `1e-8` ends at the cost of a run without them, to nine digits, in no more evaluations (`crates/fit/tests/decay.rs`).
