# sbmlsim-core 0.37.0

The global sensitivity analysis of sbmlsim runs in Rust (#137), the derived values of a fit are carried by its sensitivities (#138), and the markers of a figure are compact in PDFs, with error caps as long as matplotlib draws them (#139).

## Sensitivity analysis (#137)

The global sensitivity analysis of sbmlsim runs here: `sbmlsim_core.sensitivity`
and the crate `sbmlsim-sensitivity` draw the designs of Saltelli (on the Sobol'
sequence of scipy, scrambled), the extended FAST and Morris on the unit cube and
estimate their indices with bootstrap intervals, the designs and estimators of
SALib, every element of an output in one pass and on every CPU.

- `saltelli`, `sobol_sequence`, `fast_sample`, `morris_sample`: the designs.
  Without scrambling the Sobol' sequence is scipy's bit for bit.
- `sobol_indices`, `fast_indices`, `morris_effects`: S1, ST, S2; S1, ST of the
  extended FAST; mu, mu_star, sigma, mu_star_conf, from a seed or given resamples.
- A seed gives the same points and intervals on every platform, natively and in
  WebAssembly (xoshiro256++ and libm), but not the ones numpy draws for it.
- FAST estimates its bootstrap windows from prefix sums, 7x faster than the
  estimators of numpy of sbmlsim 0.50.

See [Sensitivity analysis](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/sbmlsim-core/sensitivity/).

## Fits (#138)

- A condition with a `derived` value no longer switches the whole fit to forward difference quotients over a step of 1e-3 when the request asks for `jacobian: sensitivities`: the sensitivities carry the derived values by the chain rule over their terms, so the Fisher information and the identifiability of sbmlsim are exact again (they were off by up to 3.4 %). The contract of a fit is unchanged.

## Figures (#139)

- **Compact markers.** Every marker of a figure is a `<use>` of the pieces of its glyph, defined once per plot in the `<defs>`. The drawing is unchanged in browsers, in the PNG (resvg) and in Typst. A PDF made by Typst draws all markers of a style as one shared object instead of one object per translucent marker. A scatter of 3000 translucent markers drops from 2.4 MB and 12000 objects to 39 KB and 31 objects, and the SVG is a quarter smaller.
- **Error caps as matplotlib's `capsize`.** `error_cap` of a curve and of bars (`curve(error_cap=)`, `bar(error_cap=)`) is how far a cap reaches to each side of its bar. A cap is `2 * error_cap` long, as matplotlib draws `capsize`; before it was `error_cap` long. To keep the caps of before, halve `error_cap`.
- **Errors of two sides in the types.** `curve(xerr=, yerr=)` and `bar(err=)` are annotated as `sbmlsim_core.chart.Errors`: one error per value, a tuple `(low, high)` or a mapping of `low` and `high`.

## Behaviour changes

- A cap of an error bar is `2 * error_cap` long, as matplotlib draws `capsize`; halve `error_cap` to keep the caps of before (#139).
- A seed of a sensitivity design gives other points and intervals than SALib's numpy draws for it (#137).
